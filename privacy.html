Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

AtoExplains-API ("the tool") is an internal script used solely by the owner of the YouTube channel Ato Explains (@atoexplains) to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and to read the owner's own channel statistics.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using the tool, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

Information accessed

With the channel owner's OAuth consent, the tool accesses only the owner's own YouTube account: uploading videos, setting thumbnails, adding videos to playlists, and reading the owner's channel, video and playlist information and statistics.

Information about other people

The tool does not collect, store, use, sell or share any personal information of anyone other than the channel owner. It has no external users, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising.

Storage

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer. Channel statistics are kept locally by the owner for personal review and are refreshed or deleted within 30 days. Nothing is sent to third parties.

Revoking access

Authorization can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation, the locally stored token stops working and is deleted.
