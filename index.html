AtoExplains-API

AtoExplains-API is a private, internal script used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Ato Explains (@atoexplains).

What it does

  • Uploads the channel owner's own short educational videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (videos.insert, thumbnails.set, playlistItems.insert).
  • Reads the owner's own channel, video and playlist information (channels.list, videos.list, playlistItems.list) and the owner's own YouTube Analytics reports (reports.query) so the owner can review performance.

OAuth scopes

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube – Manage the owner's own channel: add uploaded videos to the owner's playlists (playlistItems.insert).
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload – Upload the owner's own videos and set their thumbnails (videos.insert, thumbnails.set).
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl – Read the owner's own channel, video and playlist information over HTTPS (channels.list, videos.list, playlistItems.list).
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly – Read YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's own channel (views, watch time, likes, comments, shares, subscribers gained).
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics-monetary.readonly – Granted in the current authorization but not currently used; the tool reads no revenue data. It will be removed at the next re-authorization.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner. There are no external users, no sign-up, and no public login. The script runs on the owner's own computer and authorizes only the owner's own Google account through Google OAuth.

Data

It does not collect, store, sell or share any data about other people. See the Privacy Policy.
